About The Great Outdoors

Founded by Evan Holt, a former wilderness guide with 15+ years of experience, we're dedicated to promoting safe, responsible outdoor recreation in the Pacific Northwest.

Our mission is to help hikers discover beautiful trails while protecting the natural environment for future generations.

We believe in empowering hikers with knowledge and resources to make informed decisions about their outdoor adventures.
