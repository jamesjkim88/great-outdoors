About The Great Outdoors

Founded by Evan Holt, a former wilderness guide with 15+ years of experience, we're dedicated to promoting safe, responsible outdoor recreation in the Pacific Northwest.

Our mission is to help hikers discover beautiful trails while protecting the natural environment for future generations.

We believe in empowering hikers with knowledge and resources to make informed decisions about their outdoor adventures.

Founded by an Expert

Evan Holt is a lifelong Pacific Northwest resident and former wilderness guide with over 15 years of experience leading hikes and teaching Leave No Trace principles.

After years of witnessing both the beauty and the challenges facing our trails—from overcrowding at popular destinations to environmental degradation—Evan founded The Great Outdoors in 2024.

Our mission is simple: help people explore the Pacific Northwest responsibly while protecting the natural resources that make this region so special. Whether you’re planning your first hike or your hundredth, we’re here to guide you safely and sustainably.
