**Project Name:** Pacific Northwest Hiking Web App  
**Project Sponsor:** Poul Nichols

## **1\. Project Purpose / Background**

*(Why this project is being undertaken; business need or problem it addresses)*

* The client Founded a small organization to help people explore the outdoors  
* The organization is facing challenges that this web app could solve  
* Similar web apps are difficult to navigate or focuses more on selling gear  
* New hikers struggle to find accurate, centralized, and up-to-date trail information  
* Popular trails are overcrowded, while lesser-known trails are underused

 

## **2\. Project Objectives**

*(What the project aims to achieve; should be specific and measurable if possible)*

* Objective 1: Provide reliable and updated trail information including difficulty, distance, and elevation gain  
* Objective 2: Promote safe hiking practices and seasonal awareness  
* Objective 3: Highlight lesser known trails to prevent overcrowding  
* Objective 4: Allow Trail reviews and ratings  
* Objective 5: Allow Guided hiking reservations  
* Objective 6: Encourage responsible outdoor recreation and environmental stewardship

Success Criteria

* Creating an easy-to-navigate web app  
* Gathering and displaying up-to-date trail information in the PNW  
* Promote safe hiking and seasonal awareness  
* Provide real-time tracking for trail population

 

## **3\. Scope**

**In Scope:**

* Creating an easy-to-navigate web app  
* Promote safe hiking and seasonal awareness  
* Highlighting lesser known trails to prevent overcrowding  
* Guided Hiking registrations  
* Trail Reviews and ratings

 

**Out of Scope:**

* Collecting real-time data on user locations to assist in overcrowding prevention  
* Partnering with local parks, nonprofits, and outdoor organizations  
* Building a community around hiking in the PNW

 

## **4\. High-Level Deliverables**

*(Key outputs/products of the project)*

* Deliverable 1: UI/UX that is easy to understand and navigate  
* Deliverable 2: Information on Trails in PNW  
* Deliverable 3: User accounts to rate and review Trails
